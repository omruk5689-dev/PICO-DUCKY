Today I worked on the **Pico Ducky** schematic and board layout using **EasyEDA Pro**. The whole session took about **2 hours**, split into two parts.

**Hour 1 — Schematic & Component Placement**

Initially, I placed all the critical components on the schematic, adhering to the reference design of RP2040. The components were RP2040 processor, crystal oscillator, and QSPI flash memory. Once I placed all the critical components, I proceeded to place other peripheral components, which include decoupling capacitors, bulk capacitors, and the termination resistors for the USB interface.
